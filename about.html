# About Mount & About **Discover more. Explore local.** Mount & About is a website designed to help residents, college students, and visitors discover places to visit and things to do throughout Cincinnati and the surrounding communities. Whether you're looking for a new restaurant, a place to shop, an outdoor activity, a sporting event, or a night out with friends, Mount & About brings local options together in one convenient place. Our website organizes businesses, attractions, and entertainment into easy-to-navigate categories so visitors can explore their options without searching through multiple websites. **Our Mission** Our mission is to make exploring Cincinnati easier by connecting people with local businesses, events, and experiences. We want to encourage people to discover new places, support local businesses, and take advantage of everything the Cincinnati area has to offer. **Start Exploring** Visit our homepage to browse restaurants, shopping, parks and recreation, performing arts, sports, and 21+ activities.
